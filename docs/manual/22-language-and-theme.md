# Language and theme

## Language

LoomWright's menus, panels and messages are available in:

- English
- Deutsch (German)
- Français (French)
- Español (Spanish)
- Nederlands (Dutch)

The first time it starts, LoomWright uses the language of your computer or browser if it is one of these, and English otherwise.

To change it, choose **View › Language**, pick a language and click **Apply**. LoomWright reloads its window to switch. The projects you have open come back as they were, including unsaved changes, but the undo history starts afresh, so finish anything you might want to undo first.

What changes language, and what does not:

- Menus, buttons, panels, lists on screen and messages change.
- The words you type, such as part names, circuit names, harnesses and notes, stay as you typed them.
- Drawings (the title block and colour key), the build pack, CSV exports and the read-only web page are in English.
- The component builder and this manual are in English only.

> The translations are a first pass and will have mistakes. Corrections are welcome: see the contributing guide in the LoomWright source.

## Light and dark theme

LoomWright has a light theme and a dark theme. To start with, it follows your computer's setting. To choose one yourself, click the moon button at the right-hand end of the title bar, or choose **View › Dark theme**. LoomWright remembers your choice.

The drawing sheet stays white in both themes, so wire colours look the same as they will on paper.
