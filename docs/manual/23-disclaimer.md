# Disclaimer

Please read this before you build anything from a drawing made with LoomWright.

## A planning and drawing tool

LoomWright is a basic tool for planning wiring and drawing it. It helps you keep track of wires, colours, sizes, fuses and parts, and turns your drawing into lists for the bench. It does not design the wiring for you, and it does not know your vehicle, your cable or your installation.

## Checks and hints are guidance only

The design checks, the fuse and load figures, the wire size guide figures, the live test and every other hint in LoomWright are guidance only. They are rules of thumb based on the numbers you type in, and they can be wrong or miss things.

They do not show that a design or an installation meets BS 7671, IEC 60204, the Building Regulations or any other standard, regulation or code.

Check every wire size, fuse rating and part number against the cable, fuse and component makers' data before you build.

## Not a replacement for an electrician or engineer

LoomWright does not replace a qualified electrician or engineer. If you are not sure that a circuit is safe, ask someone who is qualified to check it.

## Mains electricity and compressed air

Work on mains electricity, and work on compressed air systems, must be designed, installed, inspected and tested by a competent person. Do not rely on LoomWright for it.
