# Disclaimer

Please read this before you build anything from a drawing made with LoomWright.

## A planning and drawing tool

LoomWright is a basic tool for planning wiring and drawing it. It helps you keep track of wires, colours, sizes, fuses and parts, and turns your drawing into lists for the bench. It is not a design service. It does not design the wiring for you, and it does not know your vehicle, your cable or your installation. It does not replace training, the standards themselves or a qualified person.

## Checks and hints are guidance only

The design checks, the fuse and load figures, the wire size guide figures, the live test, its volts and amps, fault-finding sheets, colour suggestions and every other hint in LoomWright are guidance only. So are the household and factory figures: cable ratings, derating, voltage drop, fault loop impedance, protective devices and maximum demand. They use simplified models and the numbers you type in, and they can be wrong or miss things.

A clean report does not mean a design is safe. It does not show that a design or an installation meets BS 7671, BS EN 60204-1 (IEC 60204-1), the Building Regulations (including Part P), the Electricity at Work Regulations 1989, the Pressure Systems Safety Regulations 2000 or any other standard, regulation or code. LoomWright never produces a certificate.

Check every wire size, fuse rating and part number against the cable, fuse and component makers' data before you build.

## Not a replacement for an electrician or engineer

LoomWright does not replace a qualified electrician or engineer. If you are not sure that a circuit is safe, ask someone who is qualified to check it.

## Mains electricity and compressed air

Mains wiring in homes and workplaces, machine control panels and compressed air systems can kill or injure people and start fires. That work must be designed, installed, inspected, tested and certified by a competent, qualified person, such as a registered electrician, working to the current edition of the relevant standards and the makers' instructions. Some household electrical work must be notified to building control. Do not rely on LoomWright for it.

The household and factory checks come from selected rules only. For what is checked and where each rule comes from, see [the list of standards sources](../standards-sources.md) in the LoomWright source code.

## Vehicles

Vehicle wiring has its own risks: fire from overloaded or unprotected cables, and faults that affect lights, brakes or safety systems. Check your work against the vehicle and component makers' data.
